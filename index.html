AI AI-driven Development

I use AI as an augmenting tool throughout the product and engineering lifecycle — for ideation, prototyping, documentation, and automation. AI accelerates iteration, but every suggestion is reviewed, tested, and adapted to real product constraints.

  • Human-in-the-loop: AI outputs are treated as drafts and validated by developers and tests.
  • Responsible use: Sensitive data is never sent to third-party models; privacy and security are prioritized.
  • Practical tooling: I use AI for prompt-based prototyping, code generation with review, and documentation generation.
  • Reproducible workflows: AI-assisted changes are versioned, reviewed, and integrated into CI when appropriate.
